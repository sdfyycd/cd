Grammar deep dive

🎭 一词多面 · play 的变奏曲

含义结构要点示例
玩耍；游戏及物/不及物The kids are playing in the garden.
演奏乐器必须 + theShe can play the piano. / play the erhu
球类/运动不加 theHe likes playing basketball.
扮演角色play + 角色She plays a teacher in the play.

⏳ 此刻发声 · 现在进行时

结构：主语 + am/is/are + 动词‑ing

✅ 肯定
I am playing the drums.
❌ 否定
She isn't playing the violin.
❓ 疑问
Are you playing the guitar?
📌 -ing规则
  • 🎯 一般加-ing: play→playing
  • ✍️ 去e加-ing: take→taking
  • 🔄 双写末尾: run→running, hit→hitting

✨ 修辞魅力 · 感叹句 & 反问句

🎤 What a wonderful concert!
➜ 结构：What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 + (主+谓)!
例：What a beautiful song!

❓ Isn't it a clever idea?
↳ 反问句，强烈肯定。陈述句 It is a clever idea. → 否定疑问前置。
相当于：“这主意可真聪明啊！”情感色彩更浓。
